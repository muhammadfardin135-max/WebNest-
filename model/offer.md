# The model, as it stands

**Recorded 2026-09-05**, from Muhammad's own description in session. Everything
here is his account of what he does, not a measurement of how it performs. For
performance see `numbers/`.

## What I sell

A finished website. **No build fee. €50/month, cancel anytime.**

## How the site gets made

Built **before any contact**, by outsourced people using AI. Cost to me **~$1 per
site** (my figure, see `numbers/costs.md`).

## How first contact actually works

The exact sequence, in his words — this matters, because reasoning about a
different sequence produces wrong answers:

1. The site is built.
2. A **reel** of the site is uploaded to my brand Instagram account.
3. The reel is posted as a **collab with the business**, so it appears on their
   profile too.
4. I then **DM them the forwarded reel** — not a video file attached to a message.

The brand account has **under 1,000 followers** and shows multiple websites.

The message is roughly: *we saw you don't have a site, so we went ahead and built
you one.*

## The thesis

There is nothing to imagine. The site already exists, they see it, they choose.
Full authority to say no.

## What is settled and what is open

**Settled:** the €50/month price and the no-build-fee structure. See
`decisions/pricing-monthly-no-build-fee.md`.

**Open:** everything else — segment, channel mechanics, whether pre-building
before contact earns its cost.
