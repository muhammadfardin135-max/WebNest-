# €50 per month, no build fee, cancel anytime

**Decided:** 2026-09-05 (recorded then; decided earlier, date unknown)
**The call:** Charge nothing to build the site. Charge €50/month, cancellable at
any time.
**Why:** Muhammad's stated reasoning is that €50 is a number tradesmen do not
object to, and that removing the build fee removes the barrier to saying yes when
the site is already finished and in front of them. He asked for this to be treated
as settled and taken off the table.
**What would change my mind:** `[INSERT: Muhammad has not stated a reversal
condition. Without one this cannot be revisited honestly — supply it.]`

## Evidence found after the decision, recorded here rather than argued

- **WebEngineer.ie (Dublin) sells Irish tradesmen the same offer at the same
  price:** €50/month, €0 upfront, homepage built before payment. Live pricing
  page, fetched 2026-09-05. Tier 2, primary.
- **They do not sell it cancel-anytime.** Their Starter plan carries a 12-month
  minimum with an early-exit fee of the lesser of the remaining term or €500.
  Same source and date.

The price is corroborated by a competitor in the exact segment. The
cancel-anytime term is not — an operator running this model at scale in Ireland
has concluded it cannot afford one. That is not an argument to change the price.
It is a reason to expect churn to be the thing that decides this.
